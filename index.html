Cognitive Aptitude Training Tracks

AptiVerse trains students and campus placement candidates for competitive technical hiring evaluations. The platform features 8 interactive cognitive assessment simulations:

  • Queens Challenge: Constraint-satisfaction logic puzzle inspired by algorithmic placement rounds.
  • Motion Challenge: Spatial reasoning and dynamic sliding block path optimization.
  • Geo Sudoku: Abstract geometric deduction and numerical matrix problem solving.
  • Grid Memory: Rapid visual recall and short-term working memory under timed constraints.
  • Switch & Inductive Challenge: Procedural sequence tracing and inductive pattern analysis.
  • Tab-Based Data Interpretation: Multi-tab business financial data analysis and rapid calculation.
  • Technical Reading Comprehension: Analytical textual synthesis and critical reading evaluation.

Placement Mock Tests & Company Preparation

Prepare for leading engineering recruitment drives including Infosys Systems Engineer, TCS NQT, and Capgemini gamified evaluations. Includes authentic 54-question 100-minute timed mock tests with sectional cutoff analytics, previous year questions (PYQs), and AI mock interview simulations.
